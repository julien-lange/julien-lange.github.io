I develop mathematically rigorous theories and tools that help software practitioners specify, design, maintain and implement concurrent and distributed programs. My work spans behavioural and session types, static verification of message-passing programs in languages such as Go and Rust, and the analysis of GPU kernels.

I am the Principal Investigator of INDIMO (Invariant Discovery and Monitoring for Message-Passing Programs), funded by EPSRC, 2026–2029.

Before Royal Holloway I was a Lecturer at the University of Kent and a Research Associate at Imperial College London. I hold a PhD from the University of Leicester, supervised by Emilio Tuosto, and BSc and MSc degrees from the University of Namur, Belgium.
